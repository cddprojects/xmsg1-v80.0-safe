About Us

Information on flexible work in Singapore.

Who this is for

Sider Jobs is an independent publication on Singapore career paths, industry trends and skills. It is for anyone planning their next move, or looking at a part time job, work from home in Singapore, online work or freelance jobs as a side income. We are not affiliated with any government agency.

You have limited hours, real bills and no time to sift through jargon. So each guide tells you what the work involves, what it usually looks like week to week, and what to check before you agree to anything.

How we write our guides

From your point of view

Every guide answers the questions a job seeker asks: hours, pay basis, CPF, contract and how to start.

Checked against official sources

We link to MOM, CPF Board, IRAS and MyCareersFuture so you can confirm details yourself.

Reviewed and dated

Guides show when they were last reviewed, so you know how current they are.

Articles, job type guides and tools.
