Remote, hybrid and flexible work: where Singapore stands

Last reviewed October 2026

Flexible work has moved from a perk to a standard conversation between employees and employers.

Key takeaways

  • Formal flexible work requests are now part of national guidelines.
  • Remote job Singapore listings range from fully remote to hybrid.
  • Work from home in Singapore is usually agreed in hours and place.
  • Employers can still decline a request, with reasons.
2024year national flexible work guidelines took effect
2 monthstime for an employer to reply to a formal request
3forms: flexi-place, flexi-time, flexi-load

What changed

Employers are now expected to have a process for considering flexible work requests. This does not guarantee approval, but it gives you a clear way to ask.

Remote and hybrid in practice

Remote job Singapore roles range from fully remote to a fixed number of office days. Work from home in Singapore usually means agreed hours with a Singapore employer.

What employers look for

Clear communication, reliable output, and a suitable workspace. A short trial with a review date often helps.

Many are. Check which time zone, entity and payroll apply.
