How to get started

6 steps.

  1. 1

    Choose your niche

    Focus on one or two services you can do well and explain in a sentence.

  2. 2

    Build a portfolio

    Two or three strong samples are enough to start.

  3. 3

    Decide how to price

    Hourly or per-project. Include your time for revisions, admin and tax.

  4. 4

    Find clients

    Referrals, your network, online platforms and professional profiles.

  5. 5

    Put it in writing

    Use a written agreement covering scope, deadlines, revisions and payment terms.

  6. 6

    Track income for tax

    Record every payment. You must declare freelance income.

Before you accept

Practical things to check.

Contract for services

Agree scope, deadline, revisions and payment terms in writing. Consider a deposit for larger projects.

Tax

Income from freelance work is taxable and must be declared to IRAS. Keep records of income and expenses.

MediSave

Self-employed persons may need to contribute to MediSave depending on their income. Check CPF Board.

Business registration

Depending on how you trade, you may need to register a business with ACRA. Check ACRA and IRAS.

Questions to ask

  • What exactly is included in the project?
  • How many revisions are included?
  • What is the payment schedule and due date?
  • Who owns the final work?
  • Is a deposit required?
  • What is the process if the scope changes?

Mistakes to avoid

  • Working without a written agreement
  • Underpricing and forgetting admin time
  • Not tracking income for tax
  • Taking on too many clients at once
FAQ

Common questions.

Do freelancers pay CPF?

Freelancers do not receive employer CPF contributions. Self-employed persons may need to contribute to MediSave depending on income. Check CPF Board.

Do I need to register a business to freelance?

It depends on how you operate. Check ACRA and IRAS for your situation.

How do I find freelance clients in Singapore?

Common routes are referrals, professional networks and online platforms.
