About Us

A talent resourcing practice for flexible work.

Why we exist

Part-time, remote and hybrid roles attract large numbers of applicants, and screening them properly takes time many small teams do not have. At the same time, candidates want honest guidance on roles that fit their lives.

SG Safer sits in the middle. We do the initial screening for employers and act as career consultants for candidates, so both sides meet with confidence.

How we work

Structured, not scattershot

Every candidate goes through the same criteria, so comparisons are fair.

Candidate-first ethics

Free for candidates, consent before introductions, no pressure.

Safe by default

Verified employers, verified talent, and open advice on avoiding job scams.
