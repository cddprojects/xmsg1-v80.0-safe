About Us

We make flexible work easier to understand, and harder to fake.

Why we exist

Flexible work is growing in Singapore, and so are scams built around it. People juggling caregiving, studies or a career change deserve clear answers, not another listing feed.

SG Safer explains terms, rules and risks in plain English and points to the official source every time.

What we believe

Clarity over hype

No "earn $500 a day" claims. Just what the rules and evidence say.

Verify everything

We cite official sources so you can check us too.

Neutral by design

We do not sell placements, so we have no reason to nudge you.
