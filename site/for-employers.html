For Employers

Hire flexible talent without the screening backlog.

We turn a long applicant list into a short, verified shortlist.

What you get

  • A written role brief agreed before we start
  • Structured screening interview for every shortlisted candidate
  • Availability, eligibility and reference verification
  • A one-page profile and recommendation per candidate
  • Interview scheduling with your team
  • Follow-up after start date

What we screen

Fit for the schedule

Availability, reliability and realistic commitments.

Skills and experience

Relevant experience tested with role-specific questions.

Remote readiness

Communication, tools, self-management and a suitable work setup.

Eligibility

Right to work in Singapore and, where relevant, work pass status.
