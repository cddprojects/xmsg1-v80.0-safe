Balancing Work and Caregiving: Costs and Options for Families

Last reviewed October 2026

For many parents and caregivers the question is not whether you want to work, but whether the numbers and the hours make sense.

Key takeaways

  • Start with the hours you can truly commit.
  • Ask about flexibility before accepting.
  • Subtract childcare and work costs from expected pay.
  • You can revisit your decision as circumstances change.

Start with your real hours

Write down the hours you can truly commit each week, including the school run, meals and appointments. A role that fits those hours is worth more than a higher rate that does not.

Ask about flexibility early

  • Are hours fixed or can you choose slots?
  • Can some work be done from home?
  • What happens if a child or parent falls ill?

Do the sums honestly

Subtract childcare, transport and other work-related costs from your expected pay. Sometimes fewer hours in a well-matched role beats more hours in a stressful one.

Be kind to yourself

Choosing to work part-time, or to pause, is not a failure. It is a decision you can revisit as your family changes.

Common questions

Are there jobs that fit school hours?

Some part-time and work from home roles offer hours that work around school. Ask early about flexibility.
