FAQ

Quick answers.

Are you a job board or agency?

No. SG Safer is informational only. We do not list vacancies, place candidates or take fees from job seekers.

Where can I find real part-time or remote jobs?

Start with MyCareersFuture and reputable, licensed employment agencies. Check agencies against the MOM licensed list.

Is a part-time job entitled to CPF?

Employees, including part-timers, generally receive CPF contributions subject to age, residency and pay. See CPF Board for current rates.

Can I request remote or hybrid work?

Employees can submit a formal flexible work arrangement request and employers are expected to consider it fairly under national guidelines. Approval is not guaranteed.

A recruiter asked me to pay a fee. Is that normal?

Be careful. Licensed agencies cannot charge job seekers more than the legal limits, and upfront payment to unlock a job is a scam hallmark.

How do I report a scam?

Call your bank first if money moved, then report to the Police. The ScamShield 1799 helpline can advise.

Can I send you my contract?

Remove NRIC, address and bank details first. We provide informational feedback, not legal advice.
