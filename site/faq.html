FAQ

Quick answers.

Are you a job board?

No. We are a talent resourcing and career consulting practice. We screen and shortlist for employers and coach candidates, rather than publish open listings.

Do candidates pay you?

No. Our services are paid by employer clients. Be cautious of anyone who asks a candidate for fees or deposits.

What does initial screening include?

A structured interview against the role brief, skills and availability checks, and, with consent, eligibility and reference verification.

How quickly can I get a shortlist?

It depends on the role and market. We agree an expected timeline in the brief. Figures on this demo site are illustrative.

What roles do you cover?

Part-time, remote, hybrid, contract and temporary roles, mainly admin, support, marketing, education and operations.

Is candidate data protected?

We handle personal data under Singapore's PDPA and only share a profile with an employer after the candidate consents. See our Privacy page.

Is a part-time job entitled to CPF?

Employees, including part-timers, generally receive CPF contributions subject to age, residency and pay. See CPF Board for current rates.

Can I request remote or hybrid work from my employer?

Employees can submit a formal flexible work arrangement request and employers are expected to consider it fairly under national guidelines. Approval is not guaranteed.
