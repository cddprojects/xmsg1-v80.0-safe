A CV for remote and work from home roles

Remote employers cannot watch you work, so they look for evidence that you can deliver independently.

Key takeaways

  • Show results you delivered independently.
  • List the remote tools you already use.
  • Mention time zones and availability clearly.
  • Keep it to one or two pages.

Lead with results

Use short bullets that show what you achieved on your own: tickets resolved, campaigns delivered, projects completed on time.

Show remote skills

  • Tools you already use: email, chat platforms, spreadsheets, video calls, project boards.
  • Written communication: clear, brief and polite.
  • Time management and reliability.

Be clear on availability

State your hours, time zone and any core hours you can commit to. This helps for part time job and work from home applications.

Keep it tidy

One or two pages, plain formatting and a short summary at the top. Add a link to a portfolio if you have one.

See our work from home jobs guide for roles to aim at.

Common questions

Should I mention my time zone?

Yes. State your location, hours and any overlap you can commit to.

Do I need a portfolio?

A small portfolio helps for creative, technical and content roles, but it is not required for every job.
