Upskilling in Singapore: what is available and what is worth it

Last reviewed October 2026

Training can open a new path, but only if it connects to roles you can see advertised.

Key takeaways

  • SkillsFuture credits and subsidies can lower the cost of courses.
  • Pick training tied to a role you can actually see advertised.
  • Short, stackable courses suit people who work part time.
  • Check that a course is approved before paying.
$500opening SkillsFuture Credit for citizens 25+

Modularcourses designed for working adults

Start from the role, not the course

Pick two or three job ads you would like, list the skills they ask for, then match courses to the gaps.

Funding to check

SkillsFuture Credit, mid-career support and course subsidies reduce costs. Amounts and eligibility are updated, so check SkillsFuture before you pay.

Short courses and part-time study

Many programmes are modular and offered in evenings or online, which suits people already working part time.

Avoid

  • Paying for unapproved courses.
  • Training promises that guarantee a job or income.

Looking to restart? See starting again after a break.

Common questions

Who can use SkillsFuture Credit?

Singapore citizens aged 25 and above have an opening credit. Check SkillsFuture for the latest amounts and any mid-career top-ups.

Can I study while working part time?

Many courses are modular and part-time, designed for working adults.
