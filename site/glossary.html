Glossary

Recruitment keywords, decoded.

Part-time employee
Employee contracted for fewer than 35 hours per week in Singapore.
WFH
Work from home. Sometimes written as remote or home-based.
Hybrid
A schedule that splits time between office and remote work.
Flexi-hours
Flexible start and end times, often around core hours.
FWA
Flexible work arrangement: flexi-time, flexi-place or flexi-load.
Freelancer
Self-employed person paid per project or task, responsible for own CPF and tax.
Platform worker
Person who gets work via an online platform, such as ride-hail or delivery, covered by the Platform Workers Act.
Gig
A short-term, task-based job.
CPF
Central Provident Fund. A mandatory savings scheme for employed citizens and PRs.
PMET
Professionals, Managers, Executives and Technicians.
EP / S Pass
Employment Pass and S Pass. Work passes for foreign professionals and mid-skilled workers.
SkillsFuture
National movement offering skills training credit and courses.
WSG
Workforce Singapore, an agency supporting jobseekers and career transitions.
MyCareersFuture
Government job portal operated by GovTech and WSG.
JD
Job description, listing duties and requirements.
Notice period
Time you or your employer must give before ending employment.
Probation
Initial trial period at the start of employment.
Contract / temp
Fixed-term employment, often through an agency.
AWS / 13th month
Annual wage supplement; often a bonus month paid at year end.
Money mule
Person whose bank account is used to move scam proceeds, often unknowingly. It is a criminal offence.
ScamShield
Government app and 1799 helpline that filters scam messages and provides advice.
