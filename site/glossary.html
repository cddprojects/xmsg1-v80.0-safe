Glossary

Job-ad terms, explained.

Base pay
The fixed part of your pay, before commission, bonuses or allowances.
Commission-only
Pay depends entirely on results, with no guaranteed base.
Ad hoc
One-off or irregular work, with no commitment on either side.
Retainer
A regular fee paid to a freelancer to stay available for an agreed amount of work.
Flexi-hours
Flexible start and end times, often around core hours.
FWA
Flexible work arrangement: flexi-time, flexi-place or flexi-load.
Core hours
Hours when you must be available, even in flexible or remote roles.
Hybrid
A schedule that splits time between office and remote work.
Freelancer
Self-employed person paid per project or task, responsible for own tax and contributions.
Platform worker
Person who gets work via an online platform, such as ride-hail or delivery.
Gig
A short-term, task-based job.
Contract / temp
Fixed-term employment, sometimes through an agency.
Probation
Initial trial period at the start of employment.
Notice period
Time you or your employer must give before ending employment.
Training bond
An agreement to stay for a set period after training, or repay costs.
PMET
Professionals, Managers, Executives and Technicians.
Money mule
Person whose bank account is used to move scam proceeds, often unknowingly. It is a criminal offence.
