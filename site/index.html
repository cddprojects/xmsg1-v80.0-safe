Make money at home, the safe way

Online work from home is real. So are the scams.

Searching "make money at home" brings up genuine employers and fake offers side by side. Here is how to tell them apart.

Genuine work from home job

  • You apply, or a named recruiter contacts you
  • Pay, hours and duties are clear before you start
  • A real company you can verify
  • Interviews by video or phone, a written offer
  • You are never asked to pay to get the job

Likely scam

  • Unsolicited WhatsApp or Telegram message
  • "Earn hundreds a day" for liking or rating
  • Deposits, training fees or account top-ups
  • Pressure to reply fast and keep it secret
  • Requests for Singpass, OTP or bank logins
