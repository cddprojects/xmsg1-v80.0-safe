Basics

Part-time, remote, hybrid, freelance: what each really means

Job ads mix up three separate ideas: how many hours you work, where you work, and your legal status. Untangling them is the first step.

Key takeaways

  • Hours, place and status are three different things in a job ad.
  • Part-time in Singapore means fewer than 35 contracted hours a week.
  • Freelancers manage their own CPF and tax.
  • Ask about hours, pay basis, CPF, contract type and notice period.

Hours: part-time vs full-time

Under Singapore's Part-Time Employment Regulations, a part-time employee is contracted to work fewer than 35 hours a week. Part-timers are still employees with a contract, and many protections apply on a pro-rated basis. Check MOM for the current rules.

Place: remote, hybrid, on-site

  • Remote / WFH: you work away from the employer's premises, often from home.
  • Hybrid: a mix. Ask how many office days are required and whether they are fixed.
  • On-site: you work at a workplace or client location.

Status: employee, contractor, platform worker

Employees have a contract of service. Freelancers and contractors have a contract for services and arrange their own CPF and taxes. Platform workers, such as ride-hail and delivery workers, have their own framework under the Platform Workers Act.

Questions to ask any employer: Is this an employment contract? What are my weekly hours? How is pay calculated? Is CPF payable? What is the notice period?
