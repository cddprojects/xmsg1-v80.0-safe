Part time job Singapore

Part time jobs in Singapore

Roles with fewer than 35 contracted hours a week, usually as an employee with a written contract.

Typical roles

  • Retail and F&B service crew
  • Admin and office assistants
  • Tutors and education centre helpers
  • Event and promotion staff
  • Customer service associates

Suits you if

  • You study, parent or care for family
  • You want a side income
  • You are returning to work gradually

Ask before you accept

  • Weekly hours and shift pattern
  • How pay is calculated and paid
  • CPF and leave entitlements
  • Notice period

Work from home jobs Singapore

Work from home jobs in Singapore

Roles you do from home with agreed hours, common for service and admin work. Work from home in Singapore does not have to mean freelancing.

Typical roles

  • Customer support by chat or email
  • Data entry and back-office admin
  • Virtual assistants
  • Content and social media support
  • Transcription and translation

Suits you if

  • You need to be home for family
  • You want to save commute time and cost
  • You have a quiet space and reliable internet

Ask before you accept

  • Is it an employee contract or freelance?
  • Who provides equipment?
  • Expected response times and hours
  • How performance is measured

Remote job Singapore

Remote jobs in Singapore

Roles where your location is flexible. Some are with Singapore companies, some are with overseas teams that need Singapore hours.

Typical roles

  • Digital marketing and SEO
  • Graphic and web design
  • Software and QA
  • Account and project management
  • Recruitment coordination

Suits you if

  • You have specialist skills
  • You are self-directed
  • You are comfortable with written communication

Ask before you accept

  • Which time zone and core hours
  • Who is the employer, and is it a Singapore entity?
  • Pay currency and payment method
  • Tax and CPF treatment

Online jobs

Online jobs and online work from home

Work delivered entirely online, from live teaching to moderating and research. Legitimate online work is paid by a verifiable company for defined work.

Typical roles

  • Online tutoring
  • Community and chat moderation
  • Online research and data labelling
  • E-commerce listing and order support
  • Virtual event support

Suits you if

  • You want flexible slots
  • You enjoy screen-based work
  • You can commit to reliable schedules

Ask before you accept

  • Who pays you and when
  • Whether any equipment or registration fee is requested (a red flag)
  • Training provided
  • Trial-period terms

Freelance jobs

Freelance jobs in Singapore

Project-based work where you are self-employed. You choose your clients but handle your own CPF contributions, tax and admin.

Typical roles

  • Design, illustration and branding
  • Copywriting and translation
  • Video and photo editing
  • Bookkeeping and admin support
  • Web and app development

Suits you if

  • You want control of your schedule
  • You have a portfolio or specialist skill
  • You can manage irregular income

Ask before you accept

  • Scope, deadlines and revision limits
  • Payment terms and deposits from the client
  • Contract for services in writing
  • Your own tax obligations
Read the full guide
Contract and temp

Contract and temporary jobs

Fixed-term roles for project cover, seasonal peaks or maternity cover. A practical way to rebuild recent experience.

Typical roles

  • Admin and operations cover
  • Seasonal retail and logistics
  • Project coordinators
  • Customer service surge teams

Suits you if

  • You want a defined end date
  • You are restarting after a break
  • You want to try a company or industry

Ask before you accept

  • Contract length and renewal
  • Whether it is employed via an agency
  • Leave and CPF
  • Conversion to permanent
