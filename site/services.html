Services

Support for people, and for the teams who hire them.

Friendly career consulting for candidates, careful screening for employers.

Talent resourcing

For employers hiring flexible talent.

01

Initial screening

Structured first-round interviews against your role brief: skills, availability, communication, motivation and expectations on pay and work mode.

02

Candidate shortlisting

A concise, ranked shortlist with a written assessment for each person so your hiring managers spend time only on strong matches.

03

Flexible workforce sourcing

Part-time, remote, hybrid, contract and temporary talent, including seasonal and project cover.

04

Verification and reference checks

Identity, right-to-work eligibility, qualification and reference checks, carried out with candidate consent under PDPA.

05

Role design advice

Help shaping hours, work mode, pay bands and job-ad wording so you attract the right applicants.

06

Onboarding support

Structured check-ins in the first weeks to keep part-time and remote hires engaged and settled.
