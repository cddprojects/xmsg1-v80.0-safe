Services

Guidance for seekers and employers.

We do not place candidates or charge job seekers. We explain, review and signpost.

For job seekers

Understand the offer before you accept it.

01

Offer and contract explainer

Send the terms you received (with personal details removed) and get a checklist of what to clarify: hours, pay basis, CPF, leave and notice.

02

Scam-risk review

We walk through a suspicious message or listing against known scam patterns and tell you where to verify and report.

03

Flexible-work toolkit

Templates for asking your employer about remote, hybrid or reduced hours, with talking points aligned to national guidelines.

04

Returning-to-work roadmap

A starter path for caregivers and career-returners: part-time routes, skills funding schemes and where to get free career coaching.

For employers

Design flexible roles that attract the right people.

Job ad wording review

Clear titles, work-mode labels and pay information that reduce mismatches and scam look-alikes.

Hybrid policy outline

A neutral framework covering core hours, in-office days, equipment and how requests are handled.

Compliance signposting

An orientation on part-time contracts, CPF and platform-worker rules, pointing you to official guidance.

Team workshops

Short sessions on managing remote and part-time colleagues fairly and productively.
