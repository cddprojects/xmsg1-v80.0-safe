Services

We screen, match and place flexible talent.

WorkNest SG is a talent resourcing agency. We connect job seekers with employers hiring part-time, work from home, remote, online and freelance talent.

For job seekers

Free, from sign-up to start date.

01

Talent pool registration

Tell us the work you want: part time job, work from home, remote or freelance. We record your skills, hours and pay expectations.

02

Initial screening

A short, friendly interview plus identity, eligibility and reference checks, so employers can shortlist you with confidence.

03

Job matching

We match you to employers we work with who are hiring the flexible roles you want, and put you forward with your consent.

04

Profile and interview readiness

Practical help with your CV and interview preparation, including video interviews, so you are ready to be hired.

05

Placement follow-up

We check in after you start to help settle hours, expectations and any questions.
