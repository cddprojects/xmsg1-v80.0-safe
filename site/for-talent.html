For Talent

You have a lot on your plate. Let us help with one thing.

Free, friendly career consulting to help you find flexible work that fits your life and your budget.

01

A friendly first chat

Twenty to thirty minutes about you: your experience, your commitments and what you need to earn. No judgement, no jargon.

02

Tune-up together

We polish your CV and profile side by side, and help you talk about career breaks or gaps with confidence.

03

A relaxed screening

The same conversation employers rely on, made comfortable. It lets your reliability and strengths show.

04

Introductions and support

With your consent, we introduce you to suitable openings, prepare you for interviews, and check in after you start.

Who we help

Whatever season of life you are in.

  • Parents balancing school runs and childcare costs
  • Caregivers looking after ageing parents
  • Students topping up allowance and fees
  • People restarting after a break or retrenchment
  • Mature workers wanting fewer hours, not fewer options
  • Anyone whose single income is stretched

Looking out for you

When money is tight, fake "easy income" offers are especially cruel. A genuine consultant never charges candidates or asks for bank logins. Learn the signs with our scam checker and glossary.
