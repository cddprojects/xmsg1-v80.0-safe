Remote jobs in Singapore: skills, employers and what to clarify

Remote roles tend to reward specialist skills, and many are with overseas teams. For someone based here, the questions are practical: which entity employs you, which hours overlap, and how tax and CPF work out.

Before you accept

Practical things to check.

Employer entity

CPF contributions are tied to employment with a Singapore employer. With an overseas employer or as a contractor, your position can differ. Check with CPF Board and IRAS.

Time zone

Ask which hours you must be available and how flexible they are.

Pay

Confirm currency, payment method and timing, and any conversion or bank fees.

Tax

Income from an overseas employer is still income. Check with IRAS how it is treated for you.

Questions to ask

  • Who is my legal employer and where are they based?
  • Which time zone and core hours apply?
  • Am I an employee or a contractor?
  • What currency and payment method?
  • What equipment and tools are provided?
  • How will tax and CPF be handled?

Mistakes to avoid

  • Ignoring the time zone overlap
  • Assuming an overseas contractor role includes CPF
  • Not keeping a record of income for tax
  • Skipping written terms
FAQ

Common questions.

Are remote jobs in Singapore only for tech workers?

No. Marketing, design, support, project management and content roles are commonly remote.

Do remote employers need me to be in Singapore?

Some do for tax or legal reasons and some do not. Ask early.

Do I pay tax on income from an overseas employer?

Tax treatment depends on your situation. Check IRAS or a tax professional.
