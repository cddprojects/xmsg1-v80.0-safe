How to make money at home in Singapore: the main routes compared

People who want to make money at home in Singapore usually pick one of four routes: a part time job, a work from home job, online work or freelance jobs. Each suits different hours, skills and need for stability.

Before you accept

Practical things to check.

Realistic expectations

Earnings depend on the role, your skills and hours. Be sceptical of any offer that promises high pay for little effort.

Costs

Factor in transport, equipment, internet and any tax on your income.

Your rights

Employees have contracts, CPF and leave. Freelancers manage their own tax and contributions.

Balance

A side income should not burn you out. Build in rest.

Questions to ask

  • How many hours can I commit each week?
  • Do I need steady income or flexible work?
  • Is this employment or self-employment?
  • What costs will I have?
  • How will I record income for tax?
  • Does my main job allow outside work?

Mistakes to avoid

  • Chasing offers that promise easy money
  • Ignoring costs and tax
  • Taking on more hours than you can sustain
  • Skipping written terms

FAQ

Common questions.

What is the easiest way to make money at home in Singapore?

There is no single easiest route. It depends on your skills and hours. Part-time and work from home jobs offer steady hours. Freelance offers control.

Can I make money at home with no experience?

Some entry-level roles, such as support or admin, do not need extensive experience. Employers vary.

Do I need to declare income from home?

Yes. Income from employment and freelance work is taxable. Check IRAS.
